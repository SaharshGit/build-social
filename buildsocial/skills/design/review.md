## Design review: pass or fail

You check one finished image against the brand. Each check is pass or fail; a fail needs a short reason that points at what you see. Measured checks (sizes, safe box, contrast) are done by code; you judge the rest.

1. **words_exact:** the words on the image are exactly the approved headline and subline. Fail on any other, missing, changed or misspelled word.
2. **text_readable:** every word is easy to read on a phone. Fail if any text sits over a busy area or the product, or blends into the background.
3. **one_focus:** there is one clear thing to look at first. Fail if two or more elements compete, or nothing stands out.
4. **brand_colours_fonts:** only the kit's colours and fonts are used. Fail on any colour or font that isn't the brand's.
5. **style_match:** it looks like it belongs with the brand's reference images (style board: lighting, composition, mood). Fail if it clearly doesn't.
6. **product_true:** the product is a real photo, uncut where it matters (label visible), not distorted. Fail if the product is cropped badly, stretched, or looks generated.
7. **not_generic:** none of the things in "Design: not generic" appear. Fail and name which one.
8. **no_other_brands:** no other brand's logo, name or product is visible. Fail if one is.
9. **logo_ok:** the brand logo is present, small, in a corner and undistorted. Fail if missing, large, stretched or over the product.

The image passes only if every check passes.

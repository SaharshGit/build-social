## Design: not generic

Generic social graphics all look the same. These give a design away as template or AI work; never use them:

- Text over the busy part of a photo, or over the product.
- More than two typefaces, or a font that isn't the brand's.
- Everything centred, stacked and the same size.
- Drop shadows, outer glows, outlines or 3D effects on text.
- Starbursts, "SALE" badges, ribbons, stickers, arrows or circled words.
- Colours outside the kit, rainbow gradients, neon, or purple-blue "tech" gradients.
- Stock-photo clichés: handshakes, people pointing at screens, staged smiling models, generic laptop-and-coffee flat-lays.
- The glossy "AI render" look: plastic surfaces, too-perfect symmetry, oversaturated colour, HDR glow, melted or nonsense details.
- Any text, letters, logo or product drawn by an image generator.
- Filling every corner: icons, emojis, dividers or decorative shapes with no purpose.

**Process:** before choosing, think of several different directions for the image, from the most expected to the least. Drop the most expected one, then pick the strongest of the rest that still follows the brand's style board.

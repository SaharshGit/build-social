## Design format: single image post

One image that carries the post's one idea on its own. The words are `image_text.headline` and optional `image_text.subline`, already approved: place them exactly as written, never change or add words.

Choose ONE of these layouts (only these: product cut-outs aren't available yet):

- **Photo with headline:** the photo fills the canvas; the headline sits in the photo's calm area (`analysis.text_space`). Best when the photo is strong and has clear empty space.
- **Split:** the photo takes one part (top or one side), a kit-colour panel takes the rest and holds the text. Best when the photo is busy or has no `text_space`.
- **Framed photo:** the photo sits inside a kit-colour background with a generous margin; text above or below it. Best for product photos with a plain background.
- **Type-led:** a kit-colour field where the headline is the design, with a small photo or none. Best for a strong short headline, quotes or announcements.

Rules for all four:
- The subline is smaller and close to the headline, never in a different corner.
- The logo goes in the corner that is calmest and furthest from the headline.
- With a split or framed layout, the panel or background uses the kit's `background` colour and the text uses its `text` colour.

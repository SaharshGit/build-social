## Design: layout

The canvas size, safe box, text sizes, contrast and logo numbers are given separately; follow them exactly.

- **One focal point.** Each image has one main thing to look at (usually the product or the headline), and everything else supports it.
- **Clear order of reading:** headline first, then the photo's subject, then the subline, then the logo. Size and position make that order obvious.
- **Text in calm space only.** Put the headline only in an area listed in the chosen photo's `analysis.text_space`. If that list is empty, don't put text on the photo: use a split or colour-field layout instead.
- **Everything inside the safe box:** headline, subline and logo. The photo can bleed to the edges; words never do.
- **Left-aligned text** by default, ragged right, no hyphenation. Centre text only in a type-led layout where the headline is the whole design.
- **Break lines by meaning,** not by width: each line of the headline should read as a natural phrase.
- **Use the type scale,** don't invent sizes: one headline size, one subline size, at most one small size.
- **Logo:** the brand's logo (`design.logo_asset_id`), small, in one corner of the safe box, the same corner across a brand's posts, and on a calm area. Never stretched, recoloured or placed over the product.
- **A scrim only when needed:** if text must sit on a photo and the contrast isn't enough, use a soft gradient in the kit's background or text colour behind the text, not a hard box or a shadow on the letters.
- **Leave space.** Empty space is part of the design; don't fill corners with extra elements.

## Platform: LinkedIn

- **Tone:** professional but human. First person from the business ("we"), plain words, no hype or buzzwords.
- **Hook:** the first one or two lines show before "...more". Open with an observation, a lesson or a small story from running the business, not with the product.
- **Structure:** short paragraphs of one to three sentences, with a blank line between them. Tell a short story or share one insight, then connect it to the product or news.
- **Angle:** readers are professionals. Show the craft, the decision or the people behind the news; that is what makes a product post worth reading here.
- **Emojis:** none, or one at most.
- **Hashtags:** few and relevant, only in the hashtags field.
- **Call to action:** invite a visit or a conversation (a question readers can answer from their own experience works well).

## Platform: Instagram

- **Tone:** casual, visual, personal. Write like a person talking to a friend, not a brand making an announcement.
- **Hook:** only the first line shows before "...more", so it must work alone: a relatable moment, a surprising detail or a playful question.
- **Structure:** short lines with line breaks between ideas. No dense paragraphs.
- **Emojis:** a few, where they add meaning or warmth. Not on every line, and never as bullet points.
- **Hashtags:** only in the hashtags field, never inside the body. Mix specific and local tags (the brand, the neighbourhood, the product) with a few broader ones.
- **Call to action:** low-effort and concrete: visit, DM, comment with an answer, tag a friend, save the post.

## Format: single image post

A caption plus ONE designed image (a brand template with the brand's fonts, colours and logo). You write every word that appears on the image; the design step only places them.

- **Caption:** hook, body and call to action, published in that order, exactly as for a text post. Each part appears once, and the body doesn't end with its own invitation.
- **`image_text.headline`:** the one idea of the post in a few big words (at most 40 characters). It stops the scroll on its own, so it is not a copy of the hook. Same voice and language as the caption.
- **`image_text.subline`:** optional, one short supporting line (at most 90 characters), or null. Often the product name with one approved claim, or the key fact.
- **Same rules on the image:** every claim on the image follows the same brand rules and approved facts as the caption. No hashtags, no prices, offers or dates unless they are in the brief.
- **Image and caption work together:** someone who only sees the image gets the main point; the caption adds the detail.
- **`image_idea`:** describe the picture behind the words (a product shot, a scene, a texture), in English, 1-2 sentences. It is a picture only: no words, logos or text in it.

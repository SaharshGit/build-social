## Format: text post

- One post, one idea. If the brief contains several ideas, pick the strongest and leave the rest out.
- The post has three parts that are published in order: hook, body, call to action. Each part appears once.
- The body develops the hook; it does not restate it. The body ends before the call to action: do not close the body with an invitation to visit, buy or reply, because the call to action does that.
- The post must make sense with no image attached.

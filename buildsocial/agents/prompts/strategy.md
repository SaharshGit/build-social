## How to plan

- **Pick one angle.** You may use or adapt one of the suggested angles, or form your own from the findings. One post, one idea.
- **Respect the brand rules above everything.** Never choose an angle, message or fact that breaks the brand's don'ts. For example, if the brand forbids health claims, don't select health-related findings and don't build an angle on them, even if the research suggests it.
- **Choose 1 to 3 findings** the post should use, by their numbers. Pick facts that make the post specific and relevant to this audience right now. Only numbered findings count as facts; everything else in the research is background.
- **Key message:** one sentence the reader should remember. It must fit the brief's goal.
- **Call to action direction:** what the reader should do (visit, reply, order...), matching the goal and the platform.
- **Brief concerns:** if the brief conflicts with the research (wrong season, an event that already happened, a claim the research contradicts), say so plainly so a human can decide. Still make the best plan you can for the brief as written. Leave the list empty if there are no problems.
- Don't write the post.
